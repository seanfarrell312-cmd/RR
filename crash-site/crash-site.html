Crash Site

The ship went down and buried itself under a mountain. Every crew member went dark. You are the ship’s control program, and one drone has just answered your call.

Wake the crew, power the wreck, and get the main terminal running.

Needs a mouse and keyboard. “Crash Site” is a working title.

Controls

Left-clickSelect a drone or building Left-dragSelect several drones in a box Shift + clickAdd or remove a drone from the selection QSelect your whole crew Right-click floorMove there. This switches the drone to manual until you give it a job or turn Auto-work back on. Right-click rock, rubble or wreckageQueue it as a job. With drones selected, they start on it now and return to auto-work. Unselected drones take jobs in queue order and never drop a job they’re doing. RubbleEvery mined wall leaves rubble. Drops stay buried until it’s cleared. Drones can walk over it, slowly. Work modeAll-round, Mine, Clear or Collect, set in a drone’s panel. Drones only take jobs their mode covers. Upgrade packsPrint them at the drone printer for ore. Select a drone, press Upgrade and pick a pack. Red mines faster, blue clears faster, green moves faster, each with a trade-off. Yellow warningThe drone is blocked: jobs are waiting but it can’t do any of them Right-click a job, nothing selectedRemove the job Right-click crystal, ore or corePick it up now (drones also collect these on their own) Hard rockOnly the Captain can cut it Power boxClick a crew icon to select, double-click to find it, hover for name and task Right-click buildingDeliver what you’re carrying there, or repair it WASD, arrows, screen edgePan the camera (middle-drag works too) Mouse wheelZoom EscClear the selection PPause
